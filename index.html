SCM Fanpage Poster

An internal tool of SCMdistro that automatically publishes our own short-form music promo videos to our own TikTok accounts via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

What it does

We generate lyric- and beat-synced videos from our music catalog and publish them directly to TikTok accounts that SCMdistro owns and that have authorized the tool through TikTok Login. The tool is not offered to third parties.

Which TikTok products we use

  • Login Kit — so the account owner can securely authorize the tool.
  • Content Posting API (Direct Post) — to publish our own videos to our own authorized account.
