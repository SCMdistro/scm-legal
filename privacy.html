Privacy Policy — SCM Fanpage Poster

Last updated: 7 June 2026

Data controller: SCMdistro

Contact: info@scmdistro.com

1. Who we are

SCM Fanpage Poster is an internal tool of SCMdistro used to publish our own short-form music promo videos to our own TikTok accounts via the official TikTok Content Posting API. The tool is not publicly available and only serves TikTok accounts that are owned or managed by SCMdistro and that have authorized it.

2. What data we process

  • TikTok authorization data: the access and refresh tokens and the open_id that TikTok returns after the account owner authorizes our app. These are used to publish videos on behalf of that account.
  • Basic account information: the username and avatar of the authorized account, used only to display which account a video will be posted to before posting.
  • Videos and captions: the videos we generate ourselves and the captions and hashtags we upload to TikTok.

We do not process personal data of third parties, viewers or followers, and we collect no data beyond what is needed to publish on behalf of our own account.

3. How we use data

Solely to publish videos to the authorized TikTok account and to maintain the connection to that account (refreshing tokens). We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or third-party analytics.

4. Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored locally and access-restricted on SCMdistro's own system, never in public storage or version control. Access is limited to the administrator.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share data with third parties. The only external party is TikTok itself, to which videos and captions are sent for publishing, subject to TikTok's own terms.

6. Retention

Tokens are kept while the tool manages the account and are deleted once authorization is revoked or the account is no longer used.

7. Your rights and revoking access

The account owner can revoke authorization at any time via TikTok settings (Security → Apps and websites), after which the tool can no longer publish.

8. Changes

If this tool or TikTok's requirements change, we will update this policy and revise the date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy: info@scmdistro.com.
